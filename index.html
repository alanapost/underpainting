the plan underneath your plan

Before painters add detail, they lay down an underpainting: a rough first layer that maps out where everything goes, so every stroke after it has something to build on. We do that for your website, app or project.

A small community and 1:1 project sessions for people who are experts in their own field and are building a website, app or project of their own. We help you work out what to do next, sit with you while you do it, and keep the work turning into results.

Where things tend to stall

  • You know what you want to make, but not what goes into making it.
  • You have more ideas than time, and can't tell which one matters most.
  • You keep meaning to update, post or fix things, and it keeps sliding to next week.

None of that is on you. You spent years getting good at your own field, not at how websites and apps get made. AI tools and site builders can overpaint you into a corner, or leave you with expensive or hard-to-manage gaps and risks a human would have flagged. That's where you need underpainting.

The layers you can’t see yet

You can picture the finished thing. What you can't see yet is everything underneath it, the layers nobody tells you about. Where it lives online, what privacy rules apply, how payments work, what needs to be secure, what happens when something breaks. It's hard to plan for things you don't know exist.

Sometimes the gap is just a name. You can describe exactly what you need and picture how it should work, but if you don't know it's called a "digital asset management" system, you can't look it up, compare options or ask the right questions. Think of us as a reverse dictionary: you describe it, we tell you what it's called, so you get up to speed faster.

We help you sketch those layers in first: what's involved, what order it goes in, what can wait, and where you need a specialist like a lawyer or accountant. Then we break it into pieces small enough to actually do. We help you find the words, tools and risks you didn't know were there, so you can focus on the painting.

How it works

A weekly work session · free

Once a week we meet on video, say what we're each working on, then work quietly side by side for an hour, like artists sharing a studio. (Some people call this body doubling.) It's free with sign-up.

Sign up for the work session · time to be announced

A small community

A private chat space where members share what they're working on, ask questions, and show what they've made. On our own server, with no ads and no algorithm.

1:1 project sessions and a plan you can see

Two 45-minute sessions a month. We gather up whatever notes, ideas and half-finished pieces you have, and sort them into one private online page, just for you and us: what you're doing now, what's next, what can wait, and what's done. It's the underpainting for your project: rough at first, filled in as you go, and a record of how far you've come.

You can open it any time, so you never have to hold the whole project in your head or work out where you left off. We look at it together in every session and adjust it as things change.

Who it's for

People who are good at what they do, whatever that is, and who now also find themselves running a website, app or project of their own. You know your field deeply. No technical background needed, and no pressure to become technical. You own the site, app or project, and you make the calls. It's your painting. We help with what goes underneath.

It isn't a done-for-you service. If you need hands-on help with a specific job, we can quote it separately.

Questions

Do I need to be technical?

No. You never need to open a code editor. We talk about your project in your terms, not jargon.

Will you build it for me?

That's not what membership is. You stay in charge of your own work, and we help you plan it, prioritise it and keep it moving. If there's a specific job you'd rather hand off, ask and we'll quote it.

What if my project never really "finishes"?

Most don't, and that's fine. Painters rework a canvas for years, and websites, apps and projects keep going too. The goal is steady progress toward the results you want from it.

Who runs Underpainting?

Underpainting is run by someone who went to art school, started out in tech support helping people who just needed things to work, then spent years as a project, product and program manager. At web agencies, they turned fuzzy ideas into briefs and led the teams that built the sites and apps. They've also worked with individuals and organizations of every size and kind, including a large open-source nonprofit and heavily regulated fields like security and energy. At a large consumer app, they ran software governance: matching needs to the right tools, and knowing what a tool has to meet legally, financially and for security. Along the way they learned to keep lots of moving parts going, bring in the right help as needs come up, and spot the big gotchas early. Most of all, they know how to right-size things, so you don't overbuild, and you don't paint yourself into a corner either. They also have ADHD, so they know firsthand what it's like to have solid ideas and a hard time keeping the work flowing. Art school is where the name comes from. The rest is how the plan gets made.
